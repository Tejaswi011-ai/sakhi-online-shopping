About Sakhi

Sakhi is more than a brand — it’s a celebration of India's rich textile legacy, blending traditional craftsmanship with contemporary design. Founded with a vision to empower artisans and bring timeless elegance to modern wardrobes, Sakhi is committed to sustainability, authenticity, and excellence.

Each piece is handcrafted with love and precision, using ethically sourced fabrics, natural dyes, and age-old weaving techniques. We collaborate with local artisans across India to revive traditional crafts like Banarasi weaving, hand embroidery, and kalamkari art.

When you wear Sakhi, you wear a story — a story of culture, creativity, and conscious fashion.
